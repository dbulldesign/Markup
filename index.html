Import a fixture schedule

Each row becomes a symbol tool in a new palette: its label is the fixture type, and the other columns become custom fields (shown in Revu's Comments column).

TypeRequired. The fixture type or mark (A, A1, B, X...). Also accepted: Mark, Tag, Fixture Type.
DescriptionShown in Comments and as a field.
Shapecircle, square, rect, oval, roundrect, diamond, triangle or hexagon (default circle).
Color, Fill, Text Color#RRGGBB or a colour name. Leave Fill empty for no fill.
Size, HeightSymbol size in points on the sheet (default 18); Height for rect, oval, roundrect.
SubjectOptional; default "Type A". Counts are grouped by subject.
Length, QtyOptional length of each fixture (4'-0", 4', 1.2 m) and how many per symbol; placed fixtures get it, and totals add up in counts and exports.
Any other columnManufacturer, Catalog Number, Lamp, Watts, Voltage, Mounting, Notes... become custom fields.

The first row with a Type (or Mark) column is the header. Excel (.xlsx, first sheet), CSV and tab-separated files work.

Help

Pencil and fingers

  • Apple Pencil draws with the current tool. One finger scrolls, two fingers pinch to zoom. Turn on Finger draws in Settings to draw with a finger too.
  • Hover the Pencil just above the screen (on iPads that support hover) to see where a symbol will land, which markup you are over, and what a point will snap to.
  • Press and hold the Pencil (or a mouse button) still for half a second to open the tool ring around the tip: slide onto a tool and lift, or lift and tap one. The middle button is Undo.
  • Two-finger tap: undo (on a markup: open its properties). Three-finger tap: redo. While the Pencil is on or near the screen, your palm and fingers are ignored.
  • Holding the iPad upright puts the toolbar at the bottom and the markups list in a sheet you drag up and down by its handle. Left-handed mode and larger touch targets are in Settings.
  • Pencil double-tap and squeeze work in the iPad app (Settings > Pencil chooses what they do: last tool, quick menu, eraser, undo). Web apps cannot see them; use press and hold for the quick menu, or the ` key for the last tool.

Selecting and editing

  • Select: tap a markup; drag to move; drag corner or point handles to reshape. Drag on empty space to box-select (left to right: fully inside; right to left: touching).
  • Tap a grouped markup again to pick just that one. Tap a selected text box again to edit its text.
  • The properties panel sets subject, status, comments, custom fields, colours (Line / Fill / Text), rotation, and has Group, Duplicate and Delete.

Tools

  • Shapes: circle, rectangle, polygon (tap corners, tap the first point to close), cloud, line, arrow, pen, highlighter, eraser.
  • Text: text box (drag or tap, then type), callout (press at the arrow tip, drag to the text), stamps.
  • Counts: symbol (fixed size), numbered (1, 2, 3; series bar at the top), legend and Excel export from the Markups panel.
  • Measure: set the Scale first, then length, area (with cut-outs), perimeter, angle, radius, diameter.
  • Tool Chest: save tools with their style, star favourites, arrange them, import Revu .btx tool sets.
